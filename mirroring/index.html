ScreenMirroring Support

Mirror your screen and cast photos, videos, and IPTV to your TV.

Need help? Email us at ssemism@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

To help us fix your issue quickly, please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the App version, your TV or streaming device model, and what happened.

Before contacting us, check the answers below. Most connection problems are solved by making sure your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network.

Connecting to your TV

Why can’t the app find my TV or Chromecast device?

Make sure your phone and TV are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Some guest or “isolated” Wi-Fi networks block devices from seeing each other, so avoid those. Also check that Local Network access is allowed for this app in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

My Chromecast device doesn’t show up in the device list

Chromecast devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone. Try opening the Google Home app to confirm the Chromecast is online, then restart it if it was recently set up or updated.

Does this app work over cellular data?

No. Mirroring and casting require your phone and the TV or streaming device to be on the same local Wi-Fi network, so a cellular-only connection won’t be able to find or connect to devices.

Will this work with AirPlay-only devices like Apple TV?

This app is built around Chromecast and devices that support casting over a local network, rather than Apple’s AirPlay protocol. For Apple TV, use the built-in Screen Mirroring control in iOS Control Center instead.

“Couldn’t determine this device’s Wi-Fi address” error

This appears when the app can’t find a Wi-Fi connection to use. Make sure Wi-Fi is turned on and connected to a network (mirroring doesn’t work over cellular data alone), then try again.

Screen mirroring

Screen mirroring isn’t starting, or gets stuck on “Starting”

Screen capture can briefly fail to start if another app is already recording the screen, or if the device is low on resources. Close other screen-recording apps, then try again. If it keeps happening, restart the app.

Mirroring stopped as soon as I left the app

Screen mirroring only works while this app is in the foreground. If you switch to another app, lock your phone, or receive a call, the session stops. Reopen the app and start mirroring again to resume.

The picture on my TV is laggy, choppy, or low quality

Mirroring quality depends on your Wi-Fi signal strength. Move your phone and TV closer to your router, avoid heavy network use on the same network (like large downloads), and prefer a 5 GHz Wi-Fi network if your router supports one.

There’s no sound when mirroring or casting

Check the volume on your TV, not just your phone, since many smart TVs and streaming boxes control audio independently. Also confirm your phone isn’t muted or connected to Bluetooth headphones, which would send audio there instead of to the TV.

Photos, videos, and camera

Photos or videos from my library won’t cast

Make sure you’ve granted this app access to your Photos library in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos. Large videos can also take a moment to prepare before casting starts.

The camera won’t open, or shows a blank screen

Check that this app has Camera access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera. If you’ve already granted it, try force-quitting and reopening the app.

IPTV

An IPTV channel or stream won’t load

Public IPTV streams are hosted by third parties and can go offline or get slow without notice. Try a different channel to confirm your connection works, and retry the original channel again later.

My custom IPTV/stream URL isn’t working

Double-check the link points directly to a stream file or playlist (such as .m3u8 or .m3u), not a webpage. Some links also expire after a short time or only work from certain regions.

Subscriptions

I was asked to subscribe, but I thought I already paid

Purchases are tied to the Apple ID used at checkout. If you’re not seeing your subscription, make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID, then use Restore Purchases from the subscription screen.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you originally subscribed with, open the subscription screen in this app, and use Restore Purchases. It may take a few seconds to sync with the App Store.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not from inside this app. Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, select this app, and choose Cancel Subscription. See Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Other

The app crashed or froze — what should I do?

Force-quit the app and reopen it. If it keeps happening, restart your phone, make sure you’re on the latest version of the app from the App Store, and try again. If the problem continues, email us with the details listed at the top of this page.

How does the app handle my data?

Content you cast goes directly from your device to your TV over your local Wi-Fi and is never uploaded to us. Read the full Privacy Policy.
