Privacy Policy

ScreenMirroring · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ScreenMirroring app for iPhone and iPad (the “App”), developed by Jin Hyoung Park (“we”, “us”), handles your information. In short: we don’t require an account, we don’t run our own servers, and we don’t collect, sell, or share your personal information. The only data that leaves your device for us is anonymous crash reports, described below.

1. Information we don’t collect

The App has no sign-up or login. We don’t collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or browsing history. We don’t use advertising, we don’t track you across other apps or websites, and we don’t use analytics to profile you.

2. Content you cast stays on your network

When you cast photos, videos, camera captures, or your screen, the App sends that content directly from your device to your TV or streaming device over your local Wi-Fi network. It is never uploaded to us.

  • Photos and videos. You choose items with Apple’s system photo picker, so the App can only see the items you select, never your whole library. Selected items are copied to a temporary folder on your device (photos may be converted to JPEG so the TV can display them), served to your TV, and deleted when casting ends.
  • Camera and microphone. If you allow access, the App uses the camera (and the microphone, when recording video) only to capture a photo or video that you then cast. Captures are kept in a temporary folder and aren’t saved to your library by the App.
  • Screen mirroring. When you start mirroring, the App uses Apple’s ReplayKit to capture the App’s screen, encodes it, and streams it to your TV. The recording isn’t saved and isn’t sent anywhere else. iOS asks for your permission before recording starts.
  • Local network. The App scans your local network to find compatible TVs and streaming devices (such as Google Cast devices) and runs a small temporary server on your device so the TV can play the content. This only happens while you’re using the App.

3. IPTV streams

The IPTV feature loads a public channel directory from the community-run iptv-org project (hosted on GitHub at iptv-org.github.io). When you play a channel or add your own playlist link, your device or TV connects directly to that stream’s server. Like any website, these third-party servers can see your IP address and basic request information. We don’t operate, control, or receive data from these servers, and their own privacy policies apply.

Playlists you add yourself (a name and a link) are stored only on your device and are removed if you delete the App.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. The App only checks, on your device, whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock features. Apple’s handling of purchase data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, the App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google. If the App crashes, a report is sent that may include:

  • the technical details of the crash (such as which code was running),
  • your device model, iOS version, and App version,
  • the time of the crash, and
  • a random identifier for the App installation, which lets crashes from the same installation be grouped together.

Crash reports don’t include your photos, videos, screen contents, or stream links, and we don’t use them to identify you. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms and keeps crash data for a limited period (currently 90 days).

6. Google Cast

Casting uses Google’s Cast SDK. To connect to and control your Cast device, the SDK exchanges information with that device on your local network. Google may also collect limited technical information about how the Cast feature is used, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data retention and deletion

Temporary files created for casting are deleted when casting ends, and iOS may also clear them automatically. Your own playlists stay on your device until you delete them or the App. Because we don’t have accounts, we hold no personal profile about you to delete. If you have questions about crash data related to your device, contact us and we’ll help as far as we can.

9. Children’s privacy

The App isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Since we collect so little, most requests won’t apply, but you can always contact us using the email below. We don’t sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the App gains new features. We’ll change the “Last updated” date above, and significant changes will be noted in the App’s release notes.
